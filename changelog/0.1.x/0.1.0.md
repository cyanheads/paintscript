# 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

## Added

- **Paint** — 8-band reflectance spectra decomposed from sRGB, Kubelka–Munk absorption and scattering per paint kind (`opaque`, `semi_opaque`, `transparent`, `glaze`, `medium`), mixing by parts, thinner.
- **Brushes** — `flat`, `round`, `filbert`, `fan`, `splayed`, `soft`, and `knife`, swept as bristle strands along a Catmull-Rom spline with per-strand reservoirs, pressure, pickup of wet paint, dry-brush `contact`, dabs, and stroke ends (`lift`, `taper`, `stop`, `flick`).
- **Canvas** — `smooth`, `fine_weave`, and `coarse_weave` grounds, one wet film over a dry base, relief kept through `dry()`, and lighting under a `matte`, `satin`, or `varnish` surface.
- **Views** — `color`, `squint`, `value`, `relief`, and `wet` renders of any region, with an optional grid.
- **Sandbox** — scripts run in QuickJS-ng compiled to WebAssembly, a fresh runtime per run, with time, stroke, memory, and stack limits and caller cancellation; errors report the script's line and column.
- **Script vocabulary** — `brush`, `stroke`, `mix`, `palette`, `dry`, `at`, `wet`, `height`, `canvas`, `ref`, `diff`, `show`, `log`, `rand`, `noise`, with argument checks that name the function and field; `VOCABULARY_DTS` declares it in TypeScript.
- **`Painting`** — `create`, `run`, `revert`, `replay`, `view`, `image`, `at`, `diff`. Runs are atomic (64 px tile undo), report stats, a stroke review, and at most three observations, and support `dryRun` and `expectCheckpoint`; `DEFAULT_LIMITS` sets the per-run budgets.
- **Reference images** — placed by `cover`, `contain`, or `stretch`; scripts sample color with `ref.at`, edge direction with `ref.edge`, and compare with `diff`.
- **Journal** — `journalOf`, `formatJournal`, `parseJournal`, `openJournal`, `replayJournal`: a JSON Lines record that replays a painting byte for byte, checked against the reference image's SHA-256.
- **Timelapse** — `renderTimelapse` films a journal as a looping GIF, frames spaced by brushwork.
- **CLI** — `paintscript run`, `paintscript replay`, and `paintscript vocabulary`.
- **Images** — `encodePng` and `decodePng`, with no image dependencies.
